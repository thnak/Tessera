hook: useListFocus, roving focus for lists and menus (#625)

`useListFocus(target, { orientation, typeahead })` gives a group of items
one Tab stop and moves focus among them with the arrow keys. The engine
already owned the mechanism (`Node::set_focus_group` plus
`FocusManager::move_in_focus_group`, which wraps and honours Home/End);
this lands the hook's half.

The behavior sets the group's axis from `orientation`, sets the
`typeahead` flag the manager reads, and reports `activeIndex`: the index
of the item that holds the Tab stop, or -1 when focus is outside the
group. That output is what a component needs to render
`aria-activedescendant` or a highlight without keeping a second copy of
the focus state. The index is computed by walking the group's subtree
rather than read from the manager, because the manager stores the Tab
stop as a `NodeId` in a private map and a "which index" query would put a
component concern in the scene layer.

`typeahead` is a flag on this group rather than a separate `useTypeahead`
hook: the engine's typeahead already reads the group it belongs to, so a
second hook would have to find the same group again.

Evidence: `roving_focus` (10 rules, including the arrows wrapping, Home
and End, the axis being respected, and `activeIndex` tracking focus),
`hook_manifest` (the entry's shape and its numeric output) and
`behavior_registry` (the name is registered). Spec:
`docs/specs/use-list-focus-v1.md`.
