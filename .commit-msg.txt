hook: useAnnounce, a bounded de-duplicating live-region queue (#629)

`useAnnounce({ message, channel })` announces a message to assistive
technology. The engine had no live-region support at all — no `aria-live`
on `Node`, no channel in the accessibility tree — so this lands the
mechanism as well as the hook.

The mechanism is `accessibility::AnnouncementQueue`: a bounded,
de-duplicating list the accessibility bridge drains. It is its own
structure rather than a node because an announcement has no box, is never
painted, and must survive the component that produced it: a toast that
announces "Saved" and then unmounts still has to be announced. Putting it
on a node would tie the read to whether the node was still attached.

Three rules make it safe to call from a render:

- **De-duplication.** The same text on the same channel as the last
  announcement is suppressed, both by the behavior and by the queue. A
  form that re-renders on every keystroke would otherwise announce the same
  validation message dozens of times.
- **Assertive interrupts.** An assertive announcement is delivered before
  any queued polite one, and is never dropped when the queue is full; the
  oldest polite one is dropped instead.
- **Bounded text and bounded queue.** 512 bytes and 16 entries, so a
  runaway component cannot grow memory without limit.

`Services` gains the queue, which is the third service a behavior has
needed beyond the router. The behavior writes into the window's queue
rather than owning one, which is what lets an announcement outlive its
component.

The hook does not deliver to the platform: the accessibility bridge maps a
queued announcement to UIA's `RaiseAutomationEvent`, AT-SPI's
`Announcement` and NSAccessibility's `announcementRequested`. That mapping
is the bridge's contract, and it is the next slice.

Evidence: `announcement_queue` (10 rules: ordering, de-duplication, the
caps, scoping) and `announce` (7 rules: the channel, the repeat guard, the
no-queue path, and that a queued message survives a detach). Spec:
`docs/specs/use-announce-v1.md`.
