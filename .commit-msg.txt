hook: usePressFeedback spec, and make `disabled` real (#628)

`usePressFeedback` had an implementation and tests but no contract, and its
one declared option was never read: `disabled` was in the manifest, the
analyzer accepted it, and the behavior ignored it, so a disabled button
reported itself pressed.

The spec (`docs/specs/use-press-feedback-v1.md`) states ten rules with an
evidence row each. Writing it is what surfaced the gap: rule 5 says a
disabled target never reports `pressed` while still reporting `hovered` and
`focused` (ADR-064 item 9: a disabled control stays focusable and explains
why), and nothing implemented it.

The behavior now reads `disabled` at attach and on update, and suppresses
`pressed` only. `test_disabled_suppresses_pressed_only` pins both
directions, including that clearing the option lets the press through
again.

The spec also records what the hook deliberately does not do: it does not
set `aria-pressed` from `press.pressed`, because `aria-pressed` is a toggle
state and `press.pressed` is a transient interaction; a component that
conflated them would announce a toggle that flips on every click.
